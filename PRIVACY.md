# Privacy Policy for email-agent

email-agent is a personal tool that reads the owner's own Gmail account to
label emails and save draft replies.

- It runs on the owner's own computer.
- It only accesses the Gmail account of the person who logs in.
- Email content is not sold, shared, or published. Messages from unknown
  senders may be sent to the Gemini API for classification.
- Credentials and tokens are stored locally and never uploaded.
